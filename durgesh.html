About TypeMaster

A fast, distraction-free way to measure and improve your typing speed.

⚡ How WPM is calculated

Words per minute is calculated as (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes elapsed — the standard typing‑test formula, where every 5 correctly typed characters counts as one word.

🎯 How accuracy works

Accuracy is the percentage of characters you typed correctly out of all characters you typed, updated live as you go and finalized the moment your test ends.

🔒 Your data stays local

TypeMaster requires no account. Your best WPM and best accuracy are saved securely in your browser's local storage — nothing is ever sent to a server.

🌗 Built for comfort

Switch between light and dark mode any time, on any device — the layout adapts smoothly from mobile to tablet to desktop.
